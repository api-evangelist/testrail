Choosing a test management tool can affect how efficiently your team plans, executes, tracks, and reports on testing. The right fit depends on your team structure, testing methods, integrations, reporting requirements, deployment preferences, budget, and governance needs. That decision is increasingly important as teams manage more automation alongside manual and exploratory testing.
