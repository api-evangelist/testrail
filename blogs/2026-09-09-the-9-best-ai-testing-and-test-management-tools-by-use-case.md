AI capabilities have become common across software testing tools, but the term “AI-powered” doesn’t explain what the technology does. One tool might generate unit tests, while another prioritizes regression tests, detects flaky tests, maintains UI automation, or organizes results. That distinction matters as teams increase their use of AI without always seeing consistent results.
